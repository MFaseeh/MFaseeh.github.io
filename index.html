\textcolor{accent}{\rule{\linewidth}{1.2pt}} \section{Research Statement} \setlength{\parskip}{8pt} My research focuses on making machine learning systems reliable when the data they meet differs from the data they were trained on. During my PhD at Jeju National University, I worked on multimodal and multi-source learning across several domains, including long-range object detection and depth estimation for autonomous systems, anomaly detection and forecasting for industrial energy systems, and data fusion models for materials discovery, with 26 peer-reviewed journal papers (9 as first author). My recent work turns directly to trustworthiness: conformal calibration for detecting text from unseen generative models, calibrated uncertainty and guarded deployment for forecasting under regime shifts, cross-attention fusion of multiple cues for ordinal drowsiness grading, and forgery detection that generalises across image generators. I would like to join your group because its focus on how foundation models and agentic systems adapt safely to new environments is the natural next step for my work. I want to move from task-specific models to vision-language and foundation models, and to study their reliability under distribution shift in domains such as autonomous driving, healthcare, and industrial monitoring. Your group's computing resources and the ELLIS network would let me work at a scale I cannot reach now, and I would aim to publish this work at top machine learning and computer vision venues. \section{Application Contents} \setlength{\parskip}{3pt} \begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt] \item Research Statement (this page) \item Curriculum Vitae, including referees \item Academic Transcripts \end{enumerate} \newpage % ── HEADER ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── \begin{center} {\LARGE\bfseries\color{accent} Muhammad Faseeh}\\[5pt] {\normalsize PhD in Electronic Engineering (December 2026) $\cdot$ Jeju National University, Republic of Korea}\\[2pt] {\normalsize\color{muted} Reliable Machine Learning $\cdot$ Multimodal Learning $\cdot$ Generative AI}\\[4pt] {\small \href{mailto:ifaseeh1@gmail.com}{ifaseeh1@gmail.com} $\mid$ \href{mailto:faseeh@stu.jejunu.ac.kr}{faseeh@stu.jejunu.ac.kr} $\mid$ \href{https://orcid.org/0009-0008-2375-7803}{ORCID: 0009-0008-2375-7803} }\\[2pt] {\small \href{https://mfaseeh.github.io}{mfaseeh.github.io} $\mid$ \href{https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=-C-iZsAAAAAJ}{Google Scholar} $\mid$ \href{https://github.com/MFaseeh}{GitHub: MFaseeh} $\mid$ \href{https://www.linkedin.com/in/ifaseeh/}{LinkedIn: ifaseeh} } \end{center} \vspace{-6pt} \textcolor{accent}{\rule{\linewidth}{1.2pt}} % ── PROFILE ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── \section{Research Profile} I am an AI researcher working on \textbf{reliable and trustworthy machine learning}, with a focus on \textbf{multimodal learning} and \textbf{generative AI}.
